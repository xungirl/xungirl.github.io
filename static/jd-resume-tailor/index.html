    How it works
    
      
    The rule underneath: whoever writes sees your material; whoever reviews sees only the paper.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    1
    Analyze the JD
    Hard requirements, ATS keywords, the team's real work, eligibility. It stops and asks when a hard condition fails (clearance, citizenship, dates).
    
      
    2
    Write from your bank
    It picks and rewrites only your real bullets and can mine your project repos. Every new fact must be found in a file. Your bank is backed up first.
    
      
    3
    Measure
    Scripts check for one page, extractable text, every keyword present, nothing stuffed, and no empty space left at the bottom.
    
      
    4
    Blind review
    A hiring-manager and an ATS reviewer run as fresh processes in folders that hold only the JD and the PDF. The writer revises, then a gate decides.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Use it
    
        
    Runs with the AI agent you already have: Claude Code, Codex, Grok Build, or DeepSeek. Your resume never leaves your computer except to that agent.
